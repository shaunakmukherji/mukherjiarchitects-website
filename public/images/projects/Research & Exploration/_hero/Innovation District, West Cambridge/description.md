# Innovation District, West Cambridge

## Overview
A solo master's thesis at Politecnico di Milano. In most innovation campuses, learning and working are separated: different buildings, no reason designed into the plan to cross between them. This project rejects that. Four programmatic wings — education, co-working and startups, library, and culture — are connected by sky bridges that make the gap between disciplines visible and then close it. The architecture is the mechanism for encounter, not a container for it.
