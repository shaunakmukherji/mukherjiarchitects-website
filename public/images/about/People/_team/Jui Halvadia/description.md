Jui Halvadia co-leads the architecture and interior design teams, overseeing project development, coordination and delivery across the studio. She has worked on multiple projects for international brands including Marriott, Hilton, Accor and Taj. Her approach combines an understanding of human psychology with the commercial performance of spaces.

She approaches architecture and interiors as one continuous system, where the architectural framework creates the foundation for a powerful interior experience. She uses AI to strengthen technical problem-solving by identifying issues earlier, reducing human error and limiting design risk.

She holds a master's degree in Architecture from Politecnico di Milano. Her experience with Italian design studios has further shaped her international design sensibility and approach to problem-solving.
